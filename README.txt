GOOGLE FORM SETUP
1. Create a Google Form using the structure discussed for the website.
2. Copy the published responder URL.
3. In index.html, replace:
   https://forms.gle/REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_GOOGLE_FORM_ID
   with your actual Google Form URL.
4. There is no personal email address in the website source.

Suggested Google Form fields:
- Name (required)
- Organization / Hospital
- Role
- Email (required)
- What are you interested in?
  - Apply as a tester
  - Try an available application
  - Hospital / SPD inquiry
  - Investment / strategic partnership
  - General inquiry
- Message (required)

Recommended Google Form settings:
- Do not require respondents to sign in unless needed.
- Collect email addresses if you want a reliable reply address.
- Do not display respondent summaries publicly.
- Configure response notifications to your private company/admin Google account.
